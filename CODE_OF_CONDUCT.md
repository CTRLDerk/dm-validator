# Code of conduct

DM Validator is a community tool for Delta Manager modders. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience with modding or JSON.

## Expected behaviour

- Be friendly and patient, especially with new modders.
- Keep feedback about the tool and the mod files, not about people.
- Accept that a report may be a false alarm, and that a fix may take time.

## Not accepted

- Insults, harassment or discrimination of any kind.
- Sharing other people's private information.
- Posting other people's mod files without their permission.
- Spam or advertising.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainer (CTRLDerk) by direct message on the Delta Manager Discord, or to any Discord moderator. Reports are handled confidentially.

The maintainer may remove comments, issues or pull requests that break these rules, and may block people who keep breaking them.

The rules of the Delta Manager Discord server also apply to conversations about this tool there.
